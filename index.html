Key Findings
Water Crisis
The community owes the City of Houston $7,486.25 for water charges. Water costs accounted for 53% of all spending in 2025. This is the #1 driver of our financial deficit.
Structural Monthly Deficit
Current monthly expenses (~$2,925) exceed dues income ($2,688) by ~$237/month. Without additional revenue or cost reductions, reserves will continue declining.
Low Cash Reserves
Only $6,935 in available cash. Industry standard is 6-12 months of operating expenses ($17,550-$35,100). We are well below recommended levels.
Excessive Processing Fees
Annual fee burden of $2,555 ($13.30 per household/month) includes Intuit processing ($1,759) and Stellar Bank charges ($796). That is 7.9% of dues income going to fees.

Cost Savings Opportunities
1. Reduce Intuit Processing Fees
Current annual cost: $1,759 (169 transactions)
Save $780/year ($65/month)
Set up ACH direct debit through Stellar Bank to collect dues directly. Eliminates individual Intuit charges while reducing payment friction.
2. Negotiate Stellar Bank Account Fees
Current annual cost: $796 ($25.75/month)
Save $400-796/year ($33-66/month)
Many banks offer free or low-cost HOA accounts. Get quotes from Credit Union or online banks. Even negotiating with Stellar could cut charges 50%.
3. Review Landscaping Contract (Saint Clair & Sons)
Current annual cost: $4,806 ($401/month)
Save $500-1,000/year ($42-83/month)
Industry benchmark for 16-unit HOA is $300-450/month. Request competitive bids from 2-3 vendors. Consider reducing frequency.
4. Renegotiate Trash Service (Texas Pride)
