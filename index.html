Cost Savings Opportunities
1. Reduce Intuit Processing Fees
Current annual cost: $1,759 (169 transactions)
Save $780/year ($65/month)
Recommended action: Set up ACH direct debit through Stellar Bank to collect dues directly. This eliminates individual Intuit charges while reducing payment friction.
2. Negotiate Stellar Bank Account Fees
Current annual cost: $796 ($25.75/month)
Save $400-796/year ($33-66/month)
Recommended action: Many banks offer free or low-cost HOA accounts. Get quotes from Credit Union or online banks (Mercury, Brex, Stripe). Even negotiating with Stellar could cut charges 50%.
3. Review Landscaping Contract (Saint Clair & Sons)
Current annual cost: $4,806 ($401/month)
Save $500-1,000/year ($42-83/month)
Recommended action: Industry benchmark for 16-unit HOA is $300-450/month. Request competitive bids from 2-3 vendors. Consider reducing frequency or scope (e.g., every other week instead of weekly).
4. Renegotiate Trash Service (Texas Pride)
Current annual cost: $5,843 ($487/month)
Save $400-800/year ($33-67/month)
Recommended action: Get competitive quotes from Republic Services and Waste Management. Volume discounts may be available, or consolidation with recycling/yard waste services.
5. Right-Size Property Management
Current annual cost: $6,300 ($525/month) = 19.7% of dues income
Save $1,000-2,000/year ($83-167/month)
Recommended action: Industry benchmark is 8-12% of dues. Review scope with LLA Property Partners. Can any functions be self-managed? Can scope be reduced to collections, compliance, maintenance coordination only?
6. Address Water Usage Crisis
Current annual cost: $17,880 ($1,490/month) + $7,486 balance owed
Save $3,000-6,000/year ($250-500/month)
Recommended action: Request meter audit from City of Houston for leaks. Review irrigation schedule. Consider smart irrigation controller. High usage suggests leak or billing error.
